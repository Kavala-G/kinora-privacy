Kinora Demo Playlist

A small M3U playlist of openly licensed films for trying Kinora IPTV Player without an IPTV subscription. In Kinora choose M3U URL and enter:

https://kavala-g.github.io/kinora-privacy/demo/kinora-demo.m3u

Kinora does not provide content. This playlist only links to openly licensed works hosted by the Internet Archive and to public HLS test streams.
